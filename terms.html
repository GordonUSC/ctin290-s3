01

Color Script

A sequence of images or notations showing the intended color palette and mood progression across a scene or film.

Ralph Eggleston brought the color script to Pixar in 1992 as art director on Toy Story, working in pastel on black paper because it was fast and cheap. You can read the emotional arc of a film off its colour alone, sound off, no dialogue.

Reinforced by

Arcane, Official Trailer

Piltover gold against undercity neon. The palette argues class before any dialogue does.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fXmAurh012s

02

Palette

The selected range of colors in a shot, scene, or project; establishes mood and visual unity.

A palette is a rule you set and then keep. The emotional payload arrives at the moment you break it, which is the constraint on today's build.

Reinforced by

Arcane, Official Trailer

Saturation doing emotional work, scene to scene.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fXmAurh012s

03

Warm / Cool

Color temperature: warm colors (red, orange, yellow) feel intimate and energetic; cool colors (blue, green) feel distant and calm.

Reinforced by

Arcane, Official Trailer

Temperature as allegiance. Which side of the city are you being asked to stand on.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fXmAurh012s

04

Lighting

The quality, direction, and color of illumination. Hard vs. soft light; high-key vs. low-key; motivated vs. stylized.

Reinforced by

Awaken, LoL Cinematic

Emotional information sitting in the lighting and in how materials answer it, wet metal, dust, cloth, rather than in the cutting.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zF5Ddo9JdpY

05

Saturation

How much pure hue a colour carries, from flat grey at one end to full intensity at the other. Independent of how light or dark it is.

Reinforced by

Coco, Official US Trailer

The living world is muted; the Land of the Dead is saturated to the edge of comfort. Same film, two settings of one dial.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xlnPHQ3TLX8

06

Value

How light or dark a colour is, independent of its hue. The dimension that survives when you turn the picture greyscale.

Reinforced by

Arcane, Official Trailer

Turn Zaun greyscale in your head. The class argument still reads, because it was built in value as well as in hue.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fXmAurh012s

07

High-key / Low-key

High-key lighting is bright and evenly lit with few shadows. Low-key is dominated by shadow with small, deliberate pools of light.

Reinforced by

Awaken, LoL Cinematic

Where the frame goes low-key, count what you are no longer being shown.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zF5Ddo9JdpY

08

Motivated / Stylized light

Motivated light comes from a source you can point to inside the world. Stylized light exists because the shot needs it, and no lamp in the fiction explains it.

Reinforced by

Awaken, LoL Cinematic

Find one light you can trace to a source and one you cannot. Ask what the second one is for.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zF5Ddo9JdpY

09

Hard / Soft light

Hard light comes from a small source and gives sharp-edged shadows. Soft light comes from a large or diffused source and gives gradual ones.

Reinforced by

Awaken, LoL Cinematic

Watch the shadow edges on faces and on wet metal. The edge is the tell, not the brightness.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zF5Ddo9JdpY

10

Cultural vs. universal colour

Some colour associations are learned inside a culture rather than shared by everyone. Treating a learned association as universal is the most common failure in a mood board.

Reinforced by

Disney's Encanto, Official Trailer

Name one colour choice that is cultural rather than universal, and say how you know. You will be asked this about Coco in the second half.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUts14xyjHg

Tone

The emotional or stylistic mood of a work; created through every visual and sonic choice.

Session 8 Today is the colour half of it.

Constraint

A hard limit on the palette, resolution, color depth, or geometry available to you. Constraint is not the opposite of expression, it is often the engine of it.

Session 2 Where Winds Meet sits here, which is why it is optional today.

Blue Lock (visual)

Visual technique of isolating a key object or character through color, lighting, or position to direct viewer attention.
