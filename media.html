A colour problem solved in public.

The Madrigal house runs a saturated Colombian palette, and every Madrigal is given a hue that is theirs before they speak. Watch the house shift warmth depending on whose story is being told, and notice that Bruno's absence is a colour absence too.

04

Coco, Official US Trailer

Pixar, official 1:28 11:16 · Second Text · play it twice

Palette as the line between two worlds.

Arcane splits colour by class and district. Coco splits it by world. The living world is warm, dim and domestic; the Land of the Dead is saturated to a degree that would be unbearable if you had not just spent twenty minutes in the muted version. A palette is a rule you set and keep, and the payload arrives when you break it.
